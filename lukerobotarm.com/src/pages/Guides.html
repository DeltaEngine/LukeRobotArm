Power on & connect

After assembly, attach the included 24V power supply. Clear the area of other tall objects so the initial calibration can run without hitting anything within a radius of about 0.5m/1.6ft.

Connect to the robot Wi-Fi to set up your network. Select the "Luke-<id>" network.

  • Android: Open Wi-Fi settings
  • Windows: Open Wi-Fi settings
  • macOS: Open Wi-Fi settings
  • Linux: Open Wi-Fi settings
  • iOS & Fallback: Device Settings → Wi-Fi

The configuration page at http://192.168.4.1 should open automatically, enter your Wi-Fi name and password here, the robot will restart and join your network. You will then get redirected back to the Control page, where you can select your new Luke Robot.

Connect your included (or external Android) gamepad to your Luke Base and control your robot arm directly:

  • Left Thumbstick: Base Rotation, Up/Down
  • Right Thumbstick: Arm Rotation, Hand Pitch
  • L2 / R2: Grab and Release

XYZ is the default. Press Select for rotation mode.

You can always flash the firmware again to reset your robot and start over (Wi-Fi AP will be back).
