Assembly

When you receive your robot, unpack it and follow the assembly steps below (<5min). Don't have a robot yet? Control a virtual one and then go to our shop to get a real one.

Power on & connect

After assembly, attach the included 24V power supply. Clear the area of other tall objects so the initial calibration can run without hitting anything within a radius of about 0.5 m / 1.5 ft.

Then connect to the robot Wi-Fi AP to set it up for your network. Select the "Luke-<id>" network.

  • Android: Open Wi-Fi settings
  • iOS: Open Wi-Fi settings
  • Windows: Open Wi-Fi settings
  • macOS: Open Wi-Fi settings
  • Linux: Open Wi-Fi settings
  • Fallback: Go to your device Settings -> Wi-Fi
The configuration page at http://192.168.4.1 should open automatically, enter your Wi-Fi name and password here, the robot will restart and join your network. You will then get redirected back to the Control page, where you can select your new Luke Robot.

You can always flash the firmware again to reset your robot and start over (Wi-Fi AP will be back).
