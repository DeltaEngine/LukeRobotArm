Connection

Control Luke with your voice from the browser. Speech recognition runs on your device; phrases are sent to the robot over WebSocket.

Listen

Works best in Chrome / Edge on desktop and Android. iOS Safari support is limited. Allow microphone access when prompted.

Idle
Say something like “move left”, “open gripper”, or “home”.

Example phrases

  • “Home” / “Reset”
  • “Open gripper” / “Close gripper”
  • “Move left” / “Move right” / “Move forward” / “Move back”
  • “Stop”
  • Any custom phrases your firmware or AI layer understands
