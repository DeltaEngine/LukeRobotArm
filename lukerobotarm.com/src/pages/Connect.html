Connect

Connect this browser to your Luke robot over the local network. Your phone or PC must be on the same Wi‑Fi as the arm.

First-time Wi‑Fi setup

  1. Power on Luke. It creates a Wi‑Fi access point named Luke-<id> if it cannot join a known network.
  2. Connect your phone or laptop to that AP. The captive portal should open automatically; otherwise go to http://192.168.4.1.
  3. Enter your home or workshop Wi‑Fi credentials so Luke can talk to your devices on that network.
  4. Switch back to your normal Wi‑Fi, then use the panel below to connect to Luke’s IP.

Tip: you can also open http://Luke or the IP shown on the robot display (if equipped) while on the same network.

What runs where?

The server on the ESP32 is the heart of the arm: low power, always on, and deliberately simple. It follows the instructions you send. All of the smarter UI — voice, camera, AI, and this website — runs in your browser (phone, tablet, or PC).

Developers can flash custom firmware with PlatformIO; see the GitHub repo and the Guides page for programming examples (Python, C#, JS, and more).
