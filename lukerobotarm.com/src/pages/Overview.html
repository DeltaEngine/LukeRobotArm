Why Luke?

Industry has struggled to deliver low-cost automation since robotics first arrived. Most robots are expensive and complex. Luke has a range of 0.5m/1.6ft and is still stable to solve automation tasks and affordable ($299-$599), a combination you won't find anywhere else in robotics. Whether you are a hobbyist or tech enthusiast, Luke provides ready-made high-level examples, and if you want to go deep, full low-level access too. It runs on a built-in ESP32.

  • Voice control - interact and command with your phone
  • Camera - Robot+phone cameras for object detection.
  • Markers - AprilTag circle21h7 (and ArUco) for position-aware tasks
  • Control - virtual joysticks, sliders, and gamepad support
  • Recorded moves - play back saved motion sequences
  • AI-ready - generative AI and ML workflows via this app
  • Open firmware - Customizable ESP32 you can reflash

About us

Luke is built by Delta Engine GmbH, known as game developers in the past (we created many PC, console and mobile games since the 90s). In the past 7 years we focused on robotics and AI and after creating several solutions for the industry and searching for affordable robotics solutions we decided to create a low-cost robot arm that is working and useful. Our mission is to create real affordable intelligent machines that solve real problems, not marketing hype on "humanoid" robots, which mostly never see the light of day and are used for entertainment shows and stay unaffordable.

The open-source firmware and docs live on GitHub.
