What is Luke?

Have you ever wondered how a robot would look in your home? Most robots out there are expensive or too complex. Meet Luke — your personal assistant. Integrated with generative AI, you can automate repetitive tasks with ease. Use the online controller and get started in three simple steps today.

Luke is a low-cost robot arm for your home, garage, kitchen, or workbench to help with simple tasks. It runs on a built-in ESP32 and you control it from your phone or laptop using voice, camera, gamepad, on-screen controls, or AI via this web app.

Why Luke?

Industry has struggled to deliver low-cost automation since robotics first arrived. Luke helps solve automation at an affordable price. Whether you are a hobbyist, tech enthusiast, or professional, Luke provides ready-made examples, high-level control — and if you want to go deep, full low-level access too.

  • Voice control — speak natural commands from your phone or PC
  • Camera / markers — detect ArUco markers for position-aware tasks
  • Controller — virtual joysticks, joint sliders, and gamepad support
  • Recorded moves — play back saved motion sequences
  • AI-ready — generative AI and ML workflows via the web app
  • Open firmware — ESP32 server you can reflash and customize

Quick start

  1. Connect to the Luke-<id> Wi‑Fi. The sign-in page should open automatically, or open http://192.168.4.1 and enter your local Wi‑Fi credentials so the robot can reach your phone.
  2. Rejoin your normal Wi‑Fi, then open this site and use Connect to find Luke-<id>.
  3. Once connected, control the arm with voice, camera, a gamepad, virtual controls, or AI.

On first power-up, keep the area around the robot clear. Calibration moves each joint to its extremes. On every power cycle the arm goes all the way down to re-zero — make sure nothing is underneath.
